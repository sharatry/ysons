About YSONS

Based in Bengaluru, India, YSONS bridges the gap between high-precision instrumentation manufacturing and seamless industrial implementation.

We are not just vendors; we are engineering partners. Our expertise spans the entire lifecycle of industrial process control. From consulting on the exact RTD or Thermocouple required for a high-stress thermal environment, to supplying the hardware, ensuring its calibration, and providing the robust packaging required to transport sensitive electronics safely across the globe.

Our Mission

To deliver uncompromising accuracy in process measurement while providing reliable, end-to-end service support that keeps our clients' operations running continuously.
